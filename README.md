# LexiRun: The Typing Adventure

Welcome to **LexiRun**, a dynamic and educational side-scrolling typing game built with Next.js, React, and powered by the Gemini API for endless, engaging content. Sharpen your typing skills, expand your vocabulary, and leap your way to a high score!

## 🚀 About the Game

LexiRun is more than just a typing test. It's an adventure where your keyboard is your greatest tool.

- **🎮 Core Gameplay:** Navigate a vibrant, side-scrolling world with a playful character. Run and jump using your keyboard (`A`, `W`, `D`, and `Space`) to explore the landscape.
- ** CHALLENGE:** The world is populated with floating words. Collide with one to initiate a typing challenge.
- ** TYPING MASTERY:** A modal appears, and you must correctly type the word. The game provides real-time feedback on your accuracy.
- **🧠 Educational & Dynamic:** Thanks to the Gemini API, the words and themes are constantly changing, providing a fresh experience every time you play.

## ✨ Features

- **Modern & Responsive Design:** A clean, minimalistic interface built with **ShadCN UI** and **Tailwind CSS** that looks great on any device, from mobile phones to desktops.
- **Dynamic Content Generation:**
  - **New Themes on Demand:** The game generates new word lists based on themes, ensuring you never play the same game twice.
  - **Contextual Hints:** Stuck on a word? Get a helpful hint from the AI that uses the word in a sentence related to the current theme.
  - **Vocabulary Builder:** After successfully typing a word, you can learn its definition, making the game a fun learning tool.
- **Scoring System:** Your score is based on a combination of **word length** and **typing speed**. Faster, more accurate typing on longer words yields higher points!
- **Smooth Performance:** Built on a modern React architecture, LexiRun is fast, fluid, and free of lag.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1.  **Start the Game:** Click the "Start Game" button to begin your adventure.
2.  **Move Your Character:**
    - `A` or `Left Arrow`: Move Left
    - `D` or `Right Arrow`: Move Right
    - `W`, `Space`, or `Up Arrow`: Jump
3.  **Trigger a Challenge:** Run into a floating word object to start a typing challenge.
4.  **Type the Word:** The game will pause, and a dialog will appear. Type the word you see into the input box.
    - The input border will turn **red** if you make a mistake.
    - The border will turn **green** upon successful completion.
5.  **Score Points:** Earn points for every correct word. The faster you are, the more you score!
6.  **Learn & Get Hints:**
    - Use the **Get a Hint** button if you're struggling.
    - After completing a word, click **Learn Word** to see its definition.
7.  **Next Level:** Once you clear all the words, you can proceed to the next level with a new set of words.

Have fun, and may your fingers be swift!
